Project managers shepherd projects from start to finish and serve as guides for their team, using their impeccable organizational and interpersonal skills every step of the way.

PM's play a crutail role in a organization
- prioritization
- delegation
- effective communication
### prioritization
project managers add value to their teams and organizations through effective prioritization of tasks required to complete a project

helping team members identify and break down large tasks into smaller steps

### delegation
maching tasks to individuals who can best complete the work
### effective communication
deliver value through effective communication **with the team and stakeholder**
- being transparent
- keeping contact with the team about the state of the work
- also keeping contact with people outside of the team

# how can PMs impact organizations
- focusing on the customer
	- ask the customer questions
		- what is the problem you would like us to help solve?
		- how is the problem impacting your organization?
		- what prompted you to ask for help now?
		- what is your hope for the outcome of this project?
- building a great team
	- understand each person's motivations, strengths, and weaknesses
	- identify skills needed for the project and resources available
- fostering relationships and communication
	- take time to build relationships, communicate and treat others with consideration and respect
	- set the tone for a project
	- take the time to check daily with the team
	- make people feel valued and heard
- managing the project
	- see the impact of the whole picture and how they impact other in a project
	- see the impacts of each process and communicate those impacts to the team
	- everyone should understand their task goal as well as the pic picture
- breaking down barriers
	- allow the team to innovate new ways to do things
	- empower them to share ideas
	- model ingenuity and collaboration
		- encourage the team to do the same
	- support for your team as they try new approaches and find sulutions 

### skills that may appear in a job posting

budgeting and controlling costs
- monitor and manage the budget
- track issues and risks
- manage quality
- remove unforeseen barriers, for example recognize if my team mates lack the resources to complete a tasks => identify that upfront

managing tasks:
- keep track of them
- demonstrate progress to people outside the team, such as stakeholders
