**DSL (Digital Subscriber Line)** is a broadband technology that delivers high-speed digital data transmission over existing copper telephone lines. A DSL modem/router converts the modulated telephone signal into an **Ethernet** interface for local devices, enabling simultaneous telephone voice service and internet access.

### What is DSL?
- **Definition**: Broadband technology transmitting high-speed digital communications over traditional copper local loop telephone wiring.
- **Acronym**: Digital Subscriber Line.
- **Operation**: Utilizes higher frequency bands for digital data (internet) while reserving lower frequency bands for analog voice telephony simultaneously without interference.
- **Common Types**: 
  - **ADSL (Asymmetric DSL)**: Download speeds are significantly higher than upload speeds, typical for home consumer broadband.
  - **SDSL (Symmetric DSL)**: Identical upstream and downstream bandwidth, typically used by business links.
