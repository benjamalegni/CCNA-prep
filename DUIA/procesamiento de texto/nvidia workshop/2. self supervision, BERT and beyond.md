# lab
should be go bigger?
improvements:
- sample efficiency
- larger models are cheaper to train
- architectural hyperparameters
	- large models are cheaper to design
- generalization
	- incremental improvement in accuracy, larger models generalize better

parameter efficient fine-tuning techniques (PEFT)
- adapters
	- insert into each transformer layer, only update "weights" of adapters
- LoRA. low-rank adaptation.
	- optimize rank decomposition matrices of dense layers
	- approximates a matrix by a product of two smaller matrices with lower ranks
	- choose hyperparameter r wisely
		- too small - can save a lot of parameters and memory and achieve faster training, but potentially decrease task-specific information captured in the low-rank matrices
		- too bit - can lead to overfitting
- IA3
	- like adapters, but where esach adapter is a vector that scales key, value or ffn
